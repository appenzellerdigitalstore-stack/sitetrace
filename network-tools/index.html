Network tools

Free network tools — IP, ping, DNS, and service status

Four browser-based utilities for diagnosing how the internet sees you, how your connection performs, and whether a service is up. No login, no app, no install.

Every network tool you need in your browser

SiteTrace is a small set of free network utilities that run entirely in your browser. There is nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, and nothing sent to our servers — your browser talks directly to public services. Each tool lives on its own page so you can bookmark the one you use most, share it, or come back to it whenever something on the internet feels off.

→

IP address lookup

See your public IP, approximate city, ISP, and whether your connection is exposed, behind a VPN, or routed through a proxy or Tor. Also works for any public IP — paste one in and see the registered owner and location.
→

Domain inspector (ping, IPs, latency)

Type any website and get its resolved IPs, HTTP reachability, and round-trip latency in one shot. Or add a list of URLs and watch live latency to each from your location.
→

DNS lookup

Resolve any domain to its A, AAAA, MX, TXT, NS, CNAME, and SOA records in one query. All lookups go over DNS-over-HTTPS so your queries are private, and the answers are grouped by record type for easy scanning.
→

Service status checker

Paste any URL to see if it is reachable from your network, plus live status for 50+ popular services straight from their official status pages. Includes active incidents with impact and resolution status.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Every tool runs in your browser. There is no app, extension, plugin, or download — open the page, get the answer, close the tab.

Is my data logged?

No. SiteTrace does not run a server in the middle of your lookups. Your browser talks directly to the relevant public service.

Are these tools free?

Yes. All four tools are free to use, with no rate limits, no signup, and no paywall. The site is funded by unobtrusive ads and optional donations via PayPal.

Can I use these from my phone?

Yes. SiteTrace is responsive and works on any modern browser. The IP lookup works the same on mobile as on desktop, and the service status page is fully touch-friendly.
