How it works

Every check runs directly from your browser — we don't keep logs, we don't run analytics on your data, and your IP lookups aren't tied to a session. Each request is made by your browser to public DNS, IP-geo, and service status endpoints, and the response is rendered locally.

No accounts. No tracking. No nonsense.

Frequently asked questions

What is SiteTrace?

SiteTrace is a small set of free, browser-based network utility tools: a public IP address checker, a ping and latency tester, a DNS record lookup, and a service status checker for 50+ popular sites. No account, no app to install, no data stored on our servers.

Is SiteTrace free?

Yes. All four tools are free to use, with no rate limits, no signup, and no paywall. The site is funded by unobtrusive ads and optional donations via PayPal.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Open the page, get your answer, close the tab. There is no app, no extension, no plugin, and no download.

Does SiteTrace see what I look up?

No. Your IP lookup, ping test, DNS lookup, and status checks all happen between your browser and the relevant public service. SiteTrace does not sit in the middle, does not log your requests, and does not store the responses.

Can I use SiteTrace from my phone?

Yes. The site is responsive and works on any modern browser. The IP checker works the same on mobile as on desktop, though the ping and DNS tools work best on a stable Wi-Fi or wired connection for accurate results.
